# Mascot Variation Prompts — Universal Poses

Generate each of these 6 poses for ALL 5 characters. This creates a visual system
where any mascot can appear in any emotional state — silently showing that clients
across all practice areas go through the same journey.

## Characters Reference

and say: "Match this exact 3D Pixar-style rendering for all variations."

## Pose 1: "The Weight" — Carrying the Burden

**Emotion:** Stressed, overwhelmed, but not defeated. This is the client walking in the door.

**Use on site:** Hero backgrounds, "Are you feeling this?" sections, first touchpoint pages.

```
3D rendered Pixar-style [CHARACTER DESCRIPTION], sitting down with shoulders
slightly slumped, one hand on their forehead or rubbing the back of their neck.
Expression is tired and worried but not hopeless — more "I need help" than "I've
given up." A small stack of papers or envelopes sits next to them, slightly
disorganized. [OUTFIT] slightly loosened — [SPECIFIC DETAIL: e.g., hard hat
tilted back, blazer unbuttoned, visor pushed up, glasses slipping down nose,
shoes untied]. Soft warm studio lighting, clean [BACKGROUND COLOR] background.
The character should evoke empathy, not pity. No text.
```

**Per-character notes:**
- **Sheldon**: Hard hat tilted back on his head, sitting on his shell slightly hunched, gavel set down beside him
- **Hariette**: Sitting with running shoes untied, ears drooping slightly, usually-energetic posture deflated
- **Remy**: Blazer unbuttoned, leather folder closed and set aside, tail wrapped around himself
- **Oakley**: Glasses in one hand (not wearing them), book closed on his lap, feathers slightly ruffled
- **Jasper**: Visor pushed up on forehead, glasses slipping down, clipboard face-down beside him, curled slightly inward

## Pose 2: "The Conversation" — Being Heard

**Emotion:** Attentive, warm, leaning in to listen. This is the firm hearing you out.

**Use on site:** Consultation CTAs, "How it works" step 1, contact pages, chatbot avatar.

```
3D rendered Pixar-style [CHARACTER DESCRIPTION], sitting or standing in a
relaxed, open posture. Leaning slightly forward with genuine interest. One hand
resting on their chin or gesturing gently as if saying "tell me more." Expression
is warm, focused, and compassionate — big eyes engaged and attentive. [OUTFIT]
neat and professional. A comfortable chair or simple desk setting suggested but
not detailed. Soft warm studio lighting, clean [BACKGROUND COLOR] background.
The character should feel like someone you'd trust with something personal. No text.
```

**Per-character notes:**
- **Sheldon**: Leaning forward from his shell, one hand on chin, gavel set aside — fully present
- **Hariette**: Sitting still for once, ears perked forward (actively listening), hands in lap
- **Remy**: Head tilted slightly (classic fox attentiveness), one paw gesturing "go on"
- **Oakley**: Glasses on, book closed, both wings resting on a desk, wise and patient expression
- **Jasper**: Clipboard down, visor level, looking directly at viewer with full attention, pen tucked behind ear (if worms have ears — behind glasses)

## Pose 3: "The Deep Dive" — Working Your Case

**Emotion:** Focused, determined, competent. This is the firm doing the work.

**Use on site:** "How it works" middle steps, service detail pages, loading/processing states.

```
3D rendered Pixar-style [CHARACTER DESCRIPTION], actively working with intense
but calm focus. [CHARACTER-SPECIFIC WORK ACTION]. Expression is concentrated
and determined — slight furrow of brow, mouth set with purpose. Surrounded by
a few relevant documents or tools of their trade, but not cluttered. [OUTFIT]
fully on and professional. Soft warm studio lighting with slightly dramatic
side lighting to convey focus, clean [BACKGROUND COLOR] background. The
character should project competence and dedication. No text.
```

**Per-character notes:**
- **Sheldon**: Reviewing a document with a magnifying glass, other hand holding his gavel ready, hard hat on straight
- **Hariette**: Running/moving quickly while carrying a stack of organized folders — speed WITH precision
- **Remy**: Spreading multiple documents on a surface, comparing two papers side by side, blazer sleeves pushed up slightly
- **Oakley**: Multiple books open around him, one wing pointing at a specific line in a document, glasses low on beak for close reading
- **Jasper**: Fully inside a large open book with only upper body visible, pen moving rapidly, visor pulled low for focus, glasses reflecting the page

## Pose 4: "The Breakthrough" — Good News

**Emotion:** Relief, cautious optimism, a weight lifting. The moment things turn.

**Use on site:** Progress indicators, success states, milestone sections, "What to expect" timelines.

```
3D rendered Pixar-style [CHARACTER DESCRIPTION], in a moment of genuine relief
and growing hope. Shoulders relaxing visibly, a real smile breaking through —
not forced, not manic, but the authentic relief of someone who just got good
news. One hand over their heart or reaching up as if a weight just lifted.
Eyes slightly glistening with emotion. [OUTFIT] back to neat. A document with
a visible checkmark or approval stamp nearby (no readable text on it). Soft
warm golden-hour-style lighting, clean [BACKGROUND COLOR] background. The
emotion should feel earned, not performative. No text.
```

**Per-character notes:**
- **Sheldon**: Standing taller, shell seeming lighter, hard hat in hand against chest, genuine relieved smile
- **Hariette**: Mid-bounce, one fist gently pumped, ears up and alert, the spark returning to her eyes
- **Remy**: Hand over heart, tail relaxed and flowing naturally again, a surprised-but-grateful smile
- **Oakley**: Glasses pushed up properly, wings slightly spread as if taking a deep breath, wise satisfied nod
- **Jasper**: Sitting up straight, visor pushed back proudly, holding up a document with a checkmark, wide genuine smile

## Pose 5: "The Fresh Start" — Freedom / Completion

**Emotion:** Joy, confidence, looking forward. The finish line crossed.

**Use on site:** Completion pages, testimonial sections, success stories, discharge/closing celebrations.

```
3D rendered Pixar-style [CHARACTER DESCRIPTION], standing tall and confident
with a genuine, radiant smile. Posture is open and proud — chest out, chin up
slightly, looking toward the viewer or slightly upward toward the future.
[CHARACTER-SPECIFIC CELEBRATION]. The feeling is not wild celebration but
quiet, deep satisfaction — the confidence of someone who made it through.
[OUTFIT] sharp and together. Warm, bright studio lighting with a subtle golden
glow, clean [BACKGROUND COLOR] background. This should feel like the "after"
photo — transformed but still themselves. No text.
```

**Per-character notes:**
- **Sheldon**: Standing upright and proud, hard hat held triumphantly at his side, other hand giving a thumbs up, shell polished and gleaming
- **Hariette**: Crossing an invisible finish line, arms raised in victory, running shoes bright and clean, biggest smile
- **Remy**: Blazer buttoned and sharp, folder tucked confidently under arm, free hand extended as if opening a door to the next chapter
- **Oakley**: Standing tall with book closed and tucked under wing (job done), other wing adjusting glasses with a knowing, proud smile
- **Jasper**: Emerging from the top of a book triumphantly, visor straight, glasses clean, pen held up like a tiny flag, beaming

## Pose 6: "The Friend" — Desk Pet / Always Here

**Emotion:** Warm, approachable, companionable. The ongoing relationship.

**Use on site:** Desk pet widget, chat icons, nav avatars, badge icons, favicon variants, 3D print reference, social media profile pics.

```
3D rendered Pixar-style miniature [CHARACTER DESCRIPTION], small and adorable
like a high-quality collectible desk figurine (3-4 inches tall). Sitting or
standing on a small circular base. Looking up at the viewer with a warm,
friendly expression — like a loyal companion who's always on your desk, always
in your corner. [OUTFIT] miniaturized but all details present. Pose is relaxed
and inviting — maybe a small wave, or sitting comfortably with legs dangling
off the base edge. Soft warm lighting, clean white background. Should feel like
something you'd actually want on your desk — charming, well-crafted, personal.
No text on base. No text anywhere. No nameplate. No labels. No text.
```

**Per-character notes:**
- **Sheldon**: Sitting on base with legs out, hard hat on, tiny gavel across lap, looking up with a gentle smile
- **Hariette**: Standing on base in a casual pose, one shoe tapping, tiny wave, playful grin
- **Remy**: Sitting on base edge, tail curled around the side, folder in lap, warm knowing look
- **Oakley**: Perched on a tiny stack of 2-3 books on the base, glasses on, looking down at viewer wisely
- **Jasper**: Curled around a tiny book on the base, visor on, glasses on, pen tucked behind head, looking up with studious warmth

## Generation Tips

1. **Start with one character, get all 6 poses right**, then use that as the style
   anchor for the other four. Sheldon or Hariette is best since you already have
   reference images.

2. **Upload the existing mascot image** with each prompt and say:
   "This is [name]. Generate the following pose in the exact same 3D art style."

3. **Consistency checklist** between characters in the same pose:
   - Same camera angle and distance
   - Same lighting direction and intensity
   - Same base/no base treatment
   - Same background style (solid, clean)
   - Similar body proportions (head-to-body ratio)

4. **Batch by pose, not by character.** Do all 5 characters in Pose 1, then all 5
   in Pose 2, etc. This keeps the emotional tone consistent within each pose set.

5. **No text anywhere.** Repeat "No text" in every prompt. AI image generators love
   adding text and it always looks bad, especially for 3D printing.

6. **For 3D printing:** Request a version with a pure white or transparent background
   and ask for "full body visible, no cropping, standing on a flat circular base."

## File Naming Convention

Save generated images as:
```
{character}_{pose}_{version}.png

Examples:
sheldon_weight_v1.png
remy_conversation_v1.png  
oakley_deepdive_v1.png
jasper_breakthrough_v1.png
hariette_freshstart_v1.png
sheldon_friend_v1.png
```

This gives you 30 core images (5 characters x 6 poses) that can be used
interchangeably across the entire site.
